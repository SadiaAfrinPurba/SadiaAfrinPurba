## 👋 About me

I’m a **PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Temple University**, based in Philadelphia, working at the intersection of **quantum computing, machine learning, and biomedical AI**.

My research asks a practical question:

> **When—and why—can quantum structure, especially entanglement, improve machine learning on classical data?**

I build reproducible benchmarking pipelines and quantum/hybrid models—including **QSVMs, QNNs, and QRBMs**—using real-world datasets such as EEG and digital pathology. Before my PhD, I spent five years as a Senior Machine Learning Engineer building production systems for medical coding, data quality monitoring, and retail inventory monitoring.

- 🔬 Researching quantum correlations and quantum advantage in machine learning
- 🧠 Interested in AI that is useful, explainable, and grounded in real problems
- 🛠️ Building tools that make quantum machine learning easier to experiment with
- 🤖 Long-term goal: build an AI/robotic companion inspired by Rosie from *The Jetsons*
- ⚡ Motto: **Life is beautiful!**
