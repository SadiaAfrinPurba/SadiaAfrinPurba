## 01 · Research profile

I’m a **PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Temple University**, based in Philadelphia. My work sits at the intersection of **quantum computing, machine learning, and biomedical AI**.

My central question is:

> **When—and why—can quantum structure, especially entanglement, improve machine learning on classical data?**

I build reproducible benchmarking pipelines and quantum/hybrid models—including **QSVMs, QNNs, and QRBMs**—for real-world datasets such as EEG and digital pathology. Before my PhD, I worked across machine learning, data engineering, and applied software systems.

| Focus | Direction |

| **Biomedical AI** | EEG, digital pathology, and clinically grounded learning |
